Why a graph, not a gradebook

A list of scores tells you that you failed flip-flops. It can't tell you why. Flip-flops are built on latches, latches on NAND and NOR gates, and those only click once De Morgan's theorems do.

RootCause stores the course as a graph in FalkorDB: topics linked by what comes first, and each student linked to every topic they've struggled with or mastered. Finding the root cause is a walk back along those links. Every quiz rewrites the links, so the next answer changes too.
